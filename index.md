# Gi-Woo Roh, PhD

**Research Fellow, Sogang Institute of Political Studies, Sogang University, Seoul, South Korea**

Welcome to my academic homepage. I am Gi-Woo Roh, a Research Fellow at the Sogang Institute of Political Studies, Sogang University. I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from Sogang University in 2025.

My research focuses on political behavior, political representation, party politics, youth politics, and public opinion. I have published in *Party Politics*, *Journal of Asian Public Policy*, *Journal of Youth Studies*, and *Social Science Quarterly*. My current projects examine far-right politics and political fandom, with a particular focus on South Korea.
